Claude Counter

Auto-continue

Claude Counter can send one message into a chat for you at a moment you choose: relative to the end of the 5-hour usage window (also before it), or at a fixed time, for example outside the busy hours. It sends the text "continue", or whatever is in the message box. Switch it on in the chat you want to continue in; it switches itself off after sending. The same settings are in the popup of the toolbar icon.
